## 中文版：守财奴 (God of Saving) 技术支持中心与常见问题 (FAQ)

欢迎来到 **「守财奴」(God of Saving)** 官方技术支持中心！  
*“理性克制，守住每一分自由基金。”* 如果您在使用 iPhone 或 iPad 版「守财奴」时遇到任何问题，请先查阅下方常见问题解答，或直接发送邮件联系我们。

### 一、快速操作指南与常见问题 (FAQ)

#### Q1：如何创建不同的账本（如留学账本、爱宠账本、旅行账本）并设置独立预算？
- **解答：** 在底部导航栏进入 **「账本」** 页面，点击顶部 **「小账本书架」** 按钮（或右上角 **「+ 新建账本」**），即可创建独立的专属小账本。您可以为每本账本自定义真皮装帧封面，并单独设置 **月度预算** 或 **项目总预算**，不同账本之间的预算与收支明细互不干扰。

#### Q2：如何使用「智能速记」与「多人分摊 / 报销」功能？
- **解答：**
  - **智能速记：** 点击底部中央的黄色 **「+ 记一笔」** 按钮，在顶部智能速记框中输入自然语言（例如：`午饭 38.5` 或 `打车 A$28 报销`），系统将自动匹配消费分类、币种、金额与备注。
  - **多人分摊（AA 制）：** 在记账面板中部点击 **「多人分摊/报销」** 标签，即可标记该笔账单为多人分摊（如 `3人分摊`）或待报销账目，明细列表中将自动展示醒目的蓝底分摊标识。

#### Q3：我在海外留学或定居，如何同时记录外币账单并自动折算成主货币？
- **解答：**
  1. **切换全局主货币：** 进入底部 **「我的」** 页面，在 **「全局主要计价货币」** 中选择您习惯的基准货币（支持人民币 `CNY ¥`、澳币 `AUD A$`、美元 `USD $`、欧元 `EUR €`、英镑 `GBP £`、日元 `JPY ¥`、港币 `HKD $`、新加坡元 `SGD $`、加币 `CAD $` 等）。
  2. **同步实时汇率：** 点击 **「同步实时汇率」** 按钮，应用会自动从国际金融汇率源更新最新汇率。
  3. **多币种直接记账：** 在「记一笔」页面左下角点击币种按钮（如切换为 `AUD` 或 `USD`），输入外币金额即可。系统会自动按实时汇率折算并汇总至您的账本统计与个人净资产中，同时保留原币种金额明细（如 `-¥396.63 / A$85.00`）。

#### Q4：「守财金库」中的个人净资产和股票持仓是如何计算的？
- **解答：**
  - **个人净资产公式：** `个人净资产 = 总资产（多币种资金账户余额 + 股票/基金持仓总市值） - 总负债（信用卡欠款等）`。
  - **资金账户联动：** 在「守财金库」中设置好微信钱包、支付宝、国内储蓄卡或外币银行卡的初始余额后，日常记账时选择对应扣款钱包，账户余额会自动扣减或增加；您也可以随时点击 **「设置初始/校准余额」** 一键对齐真实余额。
  - **股票与基金持仓追踪：** 点击 **「+ 新增持仓股票与基金」**，支持录入美股（如 `AAPL`）、澳股（如 `VAS.AX`）、沪深股票及 ETF（如 `510300`）。点击任意持仓标的即可随时更新最新现价与持仓股数，系统会自动按实时汇率汇总折算总市值与累计浮动盈亏（P&L）。

#### Q5：如何切换应用界面语言（纯中文 / 纯英文）？
- **解答：** 进入底部 **「我的」** 页面，在 **「界面语言设置」** 卡片中即可一键选择 **「跟随系统」**、**「简体中文」** 或 **「英文」**。切换后，全应用菜单、分类名称、账单视图、统计图表与桌面小组件将立即切换为 100% 纯正中文或纯正英文，无需重启系统。

#### Q6：如何将「守财奴」的 5 款高定小组件添加到 iPhone / iPad 桌面？
- **解答：**
  1. 先在应用内进入 **「我的」 -> 「桌面小组件中心」**，点击 **「配置与预览桌面小组件」**，选择您偏好的视觉主题（如经典黑金或 **「极简纯白」**）及想要绑定的指定账本或钱包。
  2. 返回 iPhone 或 iPad 主屏幕，长按桌面空白处进入编辑模式，点击左上角 **「+」** 号，搜索 **「守财奴」 (God of Saving)**。
  3. 从 5 款实用小组件（当天收支、指定账本、按月分类排行、指定钱包、个人金库）中挑选心仪的尺寸添加到桌面即可。

#### Q7：我的记账与资产数据安全吗？会被上传到服务器吗？
- **解答：** **绝对安全。**「守财奴」采用 **100% 本地优先架构**，您的所有账本、账单与资产净值数据仅存储在您的设备本地，我们没有任何后台服务器收集或查看您的财务数据，也绝不植入任何第三方广告追踪。

## English Version: God of Saving — Official Support & FAQ

Welcome to the official Support Center for **God of Saving (守财奴)**!  
*"Mindful spending, growing every cent of freedom."* Below you'll find quick answers to common questions about using God of Saving on iPhone and iPad.

### 1. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### Q1: How do I create separate Ledger Books (e.g., Study, Pet Care, Travel) with independent budgets?
- **Answer:** Go to the **Ledger** tab and tap **Books** in the top bar (or tap **+ New Book**). You can create dedicated ledger books for different areas of your life, customize their classic leather covers, and assign either a **Monthly Budget** or a **Project Budget** to each book independently.

#### Q2: How do Smart Quick Input and Bill Splitting work?
- **Answer:**
  - **Smart Input:** Tap the central **+ Record** button and type naturally into the top smart input bar—such as `"Coffee A$6.5"` or `"Lunch 38"`. The App automatically recognizes the category, currency, and amount.
  - **Bill Splitting / Receipts:** Tap the **Split / Receipt** chip above the keypad when logging an expense to mark a bill as a multi-way split (e.g., `3-way Split`) or reimbursable expense.

#### Q3: How do I track expenses and net worth across multiple currencies (USD, AUD, CNY, EUR, etc.)?
- **Answer:**
  1. **Set Your Primary Valuation Currency:** Go to **Profile** -> **Primary Valuation Currency** and select your preferred base currency (**USD, AUD, CNY, EUR, GBP, JPY, HKD, SGD, or CAD**).
  2. **Sync Live FX Rates:** Tap **Sync FX** under *Real-Time FX Rates* to fetch the latest international exchange rates.
  3. **Record in Any Currency:** When logging a transaction, tap the currency selector next to the amount on the keypad to choose the transaction currency. The App preserves the original currency amount on the bill while automatically converting it into your base currency for ledger totals and Net Worth calculations.

#### Q4: How does the Personal Wealth Vault calculate my Net Worth and Stock Portfolio?
- **Answer:**
  - **Net Worth Formula:** `Net Worth = Total Assets (Multi-Currency Wallets + Auto-Summed Stock Portfolio) - Total Liabilities`.
  - **Multi-Currency Wallets:** Set your Day-1 balance once for each wallet (WeChat Pay, Alipay, Bank Debit Cards, AUD/USD Bank Accounts, etc.). As you record expenses or income linked to a wallet, its balance updates automatically. You can also tap **Set / Calibrate Balance** anytime.
  - **Stock & ETF Portfolio Tracker:** In the **Vault** tab, tap **+ Add Stock / ETF Holding** to track US stocks (e.g., `AAPL`), Australian shares (e.g., `VAS.AX`), or China CSI 300 ETFs (e.g., `510300`). Tap any holding to update its share count and latest market price—the App automatically calculates your floating P&L and converts the total value into your base currency.

#### Q5: How do I switch the App language between pure English and Simplified Chinese?
- **Answer:** Open the **Profile** tab and locate the **Language Preference** card. Tap **Follow System**, **Simplified Chinese**, or **English** to switch immediately. All menus, categories, bill views, charts, and Home Screen widgets will update to 100% pure English or pure Chinese.

#### Q6: How do I add the 5 Home Screen Widgets to my iPhone or iPad?
- **Answer:**
  1. Open **Profile** -> **Home Screen Widgets (5 Practical Styles)** inside the App to configure your preferred widget theme (Classic Dark Gold or **Minimalist Pure**) and select which ledger book or wallet to pin.
  2. Long-press an empty area on your iPhone or iPad Home Screen, tap the **+** button in the top-left corner, and search for **God of Saving**.
  3. Choose from the 5 widget styles (Today's Cashflow, Pinned Ledger Book, Monthly Category Ranking, Pinned Wallet, and Personal Vault) and tap **Add Widget**.

#### Q7: Is my financial data private and secure?
- **Answer:** **Yes, 100%.** God of Saving uses a **Local-First architecture**. All of your ledger books, bills, wallet balances, and stock holdings are stored locally on your device. We never upload your financial data to external servers, and the App contains zero third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.
